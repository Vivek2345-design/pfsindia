About The Event

Experience running at Karma Lakelands at night! For a change, the 5th Sundown Run 2025 will be in winter going forward. A flat course and the route will be well-lit and traffic-free inside a secured gated estate, surrounded by the Aravali Forest and away from the hustle-bustle of crowded city roads.

Prizes & Gifts

The fastest 3 male & female runners in each timed distance category (50K, 30K, 21.1K, 10K, 5K Run) will be felicitated with trophies.

There are no cash prizes.

Age category podium finishers will be felicitated on a later date. Ranking by Net Time (chip time).

5Km Walkathon is a non-competitive, non-timed category.
