06 · Essential rules

Read before you toe the line.

These points directly affect timing, qualification, medals, safety and your race experience.

Distance change / downgrade / upgrade
  • Runners who downgrade their distance during the run, irrespective of the reason, will not be considered qualified for the lower distance or ranking.
  • If you have already decided to downgrade or upgrade, inform the Help Desk in the assembly area and start with the respective distance start time.
  • If you do not report the change before the start, you will not receive a timing certificate for the changed distance.
  • You cannot change your race distance after starting the race.
Baggage & medal counter
  • You may keep baggage in your car or deposit it at the baggage counter.
  • If baggage is left inside the car, keep it in the dashboard/boot and out of outside view.
  • Mark identification on your bag and do not leave valuables in it.
  • A separate tent will be available for ultra runners to store baggage and race-related material.
Parking & toilet facilities
  • Sufficient parking is available at the race venue.
  • Toilet facility is available at the venue.
Rewards & felicitations
  • Columbia Sports goodies for the winner.
  • No prizes/felicitation where participation is 5 or fewer in a gender category of a distance.
  • Only the winner will be felicitated where participation is between 5 and 10.
  • Top 3 will be felicitated where participation is 11 or above.
  • Age-category prizes apply only in distance categories with more than 100 participants.
Points worth noting
  • Start your run/walk only with your respective distance category.
  • Participants marked DNF (Did Not Finish) will not receive a finisher medal or timing certificate.
  • Carry your mobile phone for navigation and safety. In an emergency, contact the nearest aid station.
  • Keep your phone fully charged; GPS/navigation may drain the battery faster.
  • Long socks with shorts/lowers and a cap are advisable for trail protection.
  • Stay focused throughout the course even though portions of the trail may feel relatively simple.
